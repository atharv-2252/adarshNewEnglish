Admission Process

  1. Apply: Fill out the online registration form.

  3. Review: Application will be reviewed at the school office.
  4. Approval: Once documents are verified, admission is confirmed.

Required Documents

  • Academic transcript (Class 10th Marksheet)
  • Aadhaar Card
  • Leaving Certificate (LC / TC)
  • Passport-size photographs
